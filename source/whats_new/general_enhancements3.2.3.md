# General enhancements

HCL Verse 3.2.3 has the following general enhancements.

Beginning with HCL Verse 3.2.3, the iNotes event form will be End of Support. For more information, see [Deprecating the iNotes event form in HCL Verse 3.2.3](https://support.hcltechsw.com/csm?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0114940)
