# General enhancements

HCL Verse 3.2.2 has the following general enhancements.

## Logout of Sametime when logging out of Verse

When using Sametime integrated with Verse, if enabled by the administrator, users can be logged out of Sametime when logging out of Verse. An alert dialog appears when user logs out of Verse. After confirming the alert, the user is logged out of Sametime too. For more information, see [Enabling Sametime logout with Verse logout](../admin/configuring_integration_with_ibm_sametime.md#section_dg4_ss2_ccc).
