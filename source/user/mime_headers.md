# How to view MIME headers and MIME full content?

1. Click the ellipsis \(![this is the ellipsis icon](images/ellipsis.png)\) icon on the received messages.

       ![](images/MIME-img.png)
   
2. Click the Show MIME Header or Show MIME Full action.
3. Click Close.
