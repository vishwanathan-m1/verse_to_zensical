# How do I use mail and calendar delegation?

Whether you need to delegate someone to manage your mail and calendar, or you want to manage the mail and calendars of others, you'll find help here.

-   **[How do I delegate access to my mail, calendar, and contacts?](../user/faq_mail_delegation_access_on_prem.md)**  
Delegate access to your mail, calendar, and contacts, or calendar and contacts only, and specify the level of access.
-   **[How do I access mail and calendars delegated to me?](../user/faq_delegated_access_to_me_on_prem.md)**  
Set up {{ shortVerseProductName }} to view or manage other people's mail and calendars. The first step is to add a button to the navigation bar so you can open the delegation panel where you can add and access delegated accounts.
{% if isVerse %}
-   **[What delegation access levels are available?](../user/faq_delegation_access_levels_on_prem.md)**  
Simple access levels are available when delegating your mail and calendar in {{ fullVerseProductName }}. If you're coming from HCL Notes, you may want to know how {{ fullVerseProductName }} access levels map to HCL Notes access levels.
{% endif %}
